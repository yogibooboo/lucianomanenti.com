Free Online Card Games (... and more)

Welcome to lucianomanenti.com, where you can play the great classics of Italian card games for free, directly in your browser, with no registration and no downloads.

Every day thousands of players enjoy their games here. The site was born from a passion for programming and the love of traditional Italian card games.

My Games

Gin Rummy

The classic two-player Rummy game. Unlike Scala 40, all melds stay hidden in your hand until you knock or go Gin. A sharp battle of memory and strategy where reading your opponent’s discards is just as important as the cards you hold.

Play Rules

Scala 40 (Italian Rummy)

The classic Italian card game with sets and runs. Form combinations to get rid of all your cards. Play against 1, 2, or 3 computer opponents. You need at least 40 points to open.

Play Rules

Machiavelli

The most strategic variant of Scala 40: you can manipulate combinations already on the table, split them and recombine them to your advantage. Also available on tablet. A game that requires cunning and big-picture thinking.

Play Rules

Spider Solitaire

The famous card solitaire: arrange all columns from King to Ace. Requires patience and strategy. Try to free entire columns for more room to maneuver.

Play Rules

Klondike Solitaire

The classic card solitaire everyone has played at least once. Build the 4 foundations from Ace to King by moving tableau cards in descending alternating-color order. Draw from the stock when stuck, and complete all foundations to win. Features auto-complete once all face-down cards have been revealed.

Play Rules

Burraco

The hugely popular Italian card game, created by popular demand from our players. Play in pairs (2 vs 2) or head-to-head (1 vs 1). Form clean and dirty burracos to score points. The game is constantly being improved: suggestions and feedback are welcome.

(and not only games...) Metronome

A realistic-looking metronome with oscillating pendulum and classic pyramid design, but with electronic metronome features: selectable time signatures (2/4, 3/4, 4/4, etc.) and different tones for downbeat, beat, and subdivision. Ideal for musicians of all levels: pianists, guitarists, drummers.
