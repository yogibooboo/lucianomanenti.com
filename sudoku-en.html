How to Play

Fill the grid so that every row, every column and every 3×3 box contains all the numbers from 1 to 9, with no repetitions.

Select a cell and enter a number with the keyboard or with the buttons on the right. Numbers flagged as wrong are shown in red and counted as mistakes: the two options below the buttons choose whether to flag only obvious conflicts with the visible numbers (default) or every mistake against the solution.

✏️ Pencil: toggle notes to jot down possible candidates in a cell.
❌ Erase: clear the selected cell.
↶ Undo: undo the last move (also Ctrl+Z).
💡 Hint: highlights a cell that can be deduced from the visible numbers (or a mistake, if present) and opens a panel with three choices: Explanation shows the logical reasoning, Fill it in fills the cell, Close dismisses the hint without changing anything.

The game is saved automatically: if you close the page you can resume where you left off.
