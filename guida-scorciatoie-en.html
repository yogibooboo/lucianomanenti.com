Shortcuts — Rules and Game Guide

This game comes from a memory of my youth that is very dear to me. I invented it as a boy to play with my brothers and friends: I would take classic sheets of graph paper, grab a ruler and colored pens, and spend hours mapping out circuits full of forks, shortcuts, and sneaky traps.

Sometimes I would tape multiple sheets together to build huge, sprawling boards, where we’d spend entire afternoons rolling dice, overtaking each other on the last turn, and sharing great laughs.

Bringing this game to life today in a digital edition is a powerful rush of nostalgia for me. I truly hope it brings you the same simple, genuine fun we shared during those carefree afternoons.

Shortcuts is a strategy board game played on a graph-paper grid (3×3 block visual layout). Players roll dice and navigate their tokens along a circuit filled with forks, shortcuts, detour loops, and tricky traps that can send pieces backwards!

1. The Board and Tracks

The circuit features several distinct colored tracks:

Track Color Feature & Effect
Main Track Black / White The main 100-cell circuit leading from start (cell 0) to finish (cell 99).
Green Shortcut Emerald / Lime Green Cuts curves and loops to gain valuable lead distance.
Orange Detour Orange A longer loop that delays progress toward the finish line.
Red Trap Red A deceptive loop that sends tokens backwards toward earlier cells!
Purple Junction Purple Cross-track bridge connecting different board areas.
Teal Turbo Teal / Cyan High-speed expressway leading directly toward the final straight.
Pink & Amber Bridges Pink / Amber Rapid diagonal connectors and shortcuts.

2. How to Play

  1. On each turn, roll two 6-sided dice.
  2. Your piece moves forward by the sum of the two dice.
  3. Double Roll: Rolling doubles (e.g. 4-4, 5-5) grants an immediate extra roll!

3. Junction & Fork Rules

Junctions and forks are marked with fine directional arrows inside cells:

  • If a piece stops exactly on a cell with an arrow, on its next turn it will follow the arrow direction!
  • If a piece passes over the cell without stopping, it continues straight on its current track.
  • Heading Indicator: The triangular arrow tip on your piece shows which direction it will move on the next step.

4. Tournament Mode & Scoring System

The game features a multi-round Tournament Mode (e.g. 1000 points target):

  • Winner's Score: The first player to reach the finish line (cell 99) wins the round and receives 100 points.
  • Other Players' Scores: All other players receive points equal to the value of the last black main track cell they touched (from 0 to 98).
  • Tournament Champion: Points accumulate round by round. The first player to reach or exceed the target score becomes the Tournament Champion!

5. Game Options & Customization

Clicking the ✨ New Game button allows you to fully configure your game:

👥 Players & Participants

  • Player Count: Play with 2, 3, or 4 players on the board.
  • Humans & Bots: Set each player as a Human (👤) or Computer Bot (🤖), with customizable player names.

🏆 Tournament Target

  • 1000 Points Tournament (Default): The standard multi-round competition.
  • 500 Points Tournament: Medium duration (~5 rounds).
  • 300 Points Sprint: Fast-paced challenge (~3 rounds).
  • 2000 Points Marathon: An extended tournament endurance race.
  • Single Match: No cumulative scoring; the game ends as soon as a player crosses the finish line.

⚙️ Gameplay Variants

  • Double Roll Grants Extra Turn (Enabled by default): Rolling doubles (e.g. 4-4, 6-6) gives you an immediate bonus roll.
  • Exact Finish Required (Bounce-back): If enabled, crossing the finish line requires an exact count to land on cell 99. Any excess roll steps cause your piece to bounce backwards! If disabled, reaching or exceeding 99 wins.
  • Junction Exit Behavior:
    • Switch to intersecting track (Default): Stopping on a junction routes your piece onto the intersecting branch track on your next turn.
    • Follow own color arrow: Stopping on a junction follows the arrow associated with the track color you arrived from.

6. Controls & Live Status Bar

  • Top-Left: Displays current match number and real-time horizontal cumulative tournament scores.
  • Top-Right: Displays the value of the last black main track cell touched by each player in the active round.
  • Auto-play (Bottom bar): Checkbox to run all dice rolls and moves automatically, ideal for watching bot matches or fast simulations.
  • Audio (Top-Left): Toggle sound effects on or off.
  • Live Chronicle (Bottom-Left): Real-time event log recording all rolls, junction events, and victories.
