Machiavelli Rules

Machiavelli is a strategic variant of Scala 40 (Italian Rummy) where, in addition to laying down your own combinations, you can manipulate the combinations already on the table. This makes it one of the most stimulating and tactical card games.

Differences from Scala 40

  • Combinations already on the table can be split, recombined, and modified.
  • In runs, after the King you can continue with Ace, 2, 3 (e.g. 10, J, Q, K, A, 2, 3).
  • If during your turn you add at least one card to the table combinations, you don't have to draw.
  • If you can't play any card, you automatically draw from the deck.

Objective

As in Scala 40, the goal is to get rid of all the cards in your hand. The difference is that you have much more freedom to manipulate the table to your advantage.

How to play "Machiavelli Luciano"

  • Form combinations: click on the cards (which MUST BE IN THE CORRECT ORDER), then click in the area above the player to lay them down.
  • Add to a set: drag the card to the desired position.
  • Split a set: click (right or left button) on a card in a table combination to split it at that point (the selected card will remain in the right part of the combination, unless it is the first card or a card in a four-of-a-kind, in which case it will be isolated).
  • Move cards: after splitting a set, you can add, move and recombine cards freely.
  • End turn: click on the free area of your field or on the deck. If during your turn you have added at least one card or combination to the field, the game will pass to the next opponent without drawing; otherwise, a card will be automatically drawn.

Buttons

  • ↩: undo the most recent moves.
  • UNCOVER: play with open cards.
  • PLAYERS: choose the number of opponents (1, 2, or 3) and/or start a new game.
  • GROUP: automatically group combinations that can be joined.
  • STEPBYSTEP: view opponents' moves one by one.
  • 🔊 / 🔇 (Audio): enables or disables the game sound effects. Your preference is saved and applied across all games on the site. Note: if your browser or operating system has muted audio for this tab, sounds will remain silent regardless of this button, as that control is managed by the browser outside the scope of the page.
