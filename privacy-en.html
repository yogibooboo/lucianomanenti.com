Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to me. This policy explains how information is managed on lucianomanenti.com in accordance with EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR).

1. Data Controller and Data Collection

The data controller is Luciano Manenti (postmaster@lucianomanenti.com).

This site is a free, non-commercial collection of static tools and games: there are no accounts, registrations, contact forms or user databases on the site's servers, and your game preferences are stored only in your own browser.

The site does not display advertisements. It uses Google Analytics 4 for visit and game statistics, as described in the sections below.

The site uses third-party services — Google AdSense for advertising and Google Analytics for visit statistics — which process some data relating to your visit, as described in the sections below.

2. Cookies and Browser Local Storage

2. Cookies, Local Storage and Advertising

Technical browser storage (localStorage / sessionStorage). To work properly, the games save certain technical preferences solely on your device (such as sound effects mute state, chosen variants, current game state or personal best scores on your device). This data never leaves your browser and is never transmitted to the server.

No advertising cookies. This site does not display advertisements and does not use advertising or profiling cookies. The only cookies set are those of Google Analytics 4 (_ga, _ga_*), described in section 3.

Advertising (Google AdSense). I use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.

In order to select the ads to display, Google shares certain technical information about the visit with its advertising partners: device and browser type, page visited, approximate location data and IP address. This information may be used to personalise ads and to measure their performance.

If you do not consent to personalised advertising, Google serves non-personalised ads and limits the information shared accordingly. The full list of advertising partners, and your choices for each purpose, can be reviewed and changed at any time from the Manage Cookies panel, available at the bottom of every page of the site.

3. Visit and Game Statistics (Google Analytics 4)

I use Google Analytics 4 to find out how much the site and each game are used, and to improve them. The service collects technical information about your visit: browser and device type, approximate location, pages visited, games started and finished with their outcome and duration, and any errors that occur while the pages are running.

The browser identifier. Google Analytics assigns each browser a random identifier, stored in the _ga cookie. It is used to tell one visitor from another and to count visits and games; the same browser is recognised when it comes back. It does not contain your name, your email address or any other data that would reveal who you are.

Data linked to this identifier is kept for 14 months. According to Google, Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. The Google Signals feature is turned off. The data is processed by Google, which may also handle it on servers located outside the European Union.

Fonts. Some pages load their fonts from Google Fonts: to do so, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive its IP address.

4. Managing Cookies and Privacy Choices

You can block or delete cookies and local storage data at any time directly from your browser settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on provided by Google.

You can manage or withdraw your consent preferences at any time through the Manage Cookies link, available at the bottom of every page of the site next to the link to this policy. The panel that opens lets you choose purpose by purpose and partner by partner. You can also opt out of Google Analytics via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

For more information on how Google processes data, see the Google Privacy Policy and the page How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Your Rights

Under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access your personal data, to request its rectification or erasure, to object to processing and to withdraw any consent you may have given. For requests concerning data processed by Google, please contact Google directly through the links in the previous section.

You are also entitled to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it).

6. Contact

For any questions regarding this policy, you can contact me at: postmaster@lucianomanenti.com
