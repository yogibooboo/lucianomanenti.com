Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to me. This policy explains how information is managed on lucianomanenti.com.

1. Data Collection

This site is a collection of static tools and games: there are no accounts, registrations or contact forms, and your game preferences are stored only in your own browser. The only archive I maintain directly is the gameplay statistics described in section 3, which contains neither your IP address nor any data that directly identifies you.

The site does, however, use third-party services — Google AdSense for advertising and Google Analytics for statistics — which process some data relating to your visit, as described in the sections below.

2. Cookies and Advertising

I use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.

In order to select the ads to display, Google shares certain technical information about the visit with its advertising partners: device and browser type, page visited, approximate location data and IP address. This information may be used to personalise ads and to measure their performance.

If you do not consent to personalised advertising, Google serves non-personalised ads and limits the information shared accordingly. The full list of advertising partners, and your choices for each purpose, can be reviewed and changed at any time from the Manage Cookies panel, available at the bottom of every page of the site.

3. Analytics and Gameplay Statistics

Google Analytics. I use Google Analytics to monitor site traffic and improve the user experience. The service collects information such as your IP address, browser type, device and pages visited. Under European law the IP address is considered personal data; Google Analytics 4 uses it to determine approximate location and does not store it in the reports.

Gameplay statistics. Some games send the outcome of each completed game to my server (result, score, duration and game configuration) in order to assess how well the game algorithms perform. Your IP address is not recorded, and the outcome of a game contains nothing that concerns you: it is the bare facts of a hand of cards.

If you have given consent for the relevant purposes in the Manage Cookies panel, a random identifier is added to the result: your browser generates it once and keeps it internally, and it makes it possible to recognise games coming from the same browser — not from the same person. It is not shared with anyone and is not used for advertising purposes. You can withdraw your consent at any time from the same panel: the identifier is deleted from your browser and later games are recorded without it, that is, anonymously. The data is kept only for as long as is strictly necessary to compare the game algorithms, and is then deleted.

4. Managing Privacy Choices

You can manage or withdraw your consent preferences at any time through the Manage Cookies link, available at the bottom of every page of the site next to the link to this policy. The panel that opens lets you choose purpose by purpose and partner by partner.

For more information on how Google processes data, see the Google Privacy Policy and the page How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Your Rights

Under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access your personal data, to request its rectification or erasure, to object to processing and to withdraw any consent you have given. For requests concerning data processed by Google, please contact Google directly through the links in the previous section.

The gameplay statistics described in section 3 contain no data that identifies you, and I have no means of tracing them back to you. By withdrawing your consent from the Manage Cookies panel, the identifier kept in your browser is deleted and later games are recorded anonymously.

You are also entitled to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

6. Contact

For any questions regarding this policy, you can contact me at: postmaster@lucianomanenti.com
