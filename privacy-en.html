Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to me. This policy explains how information is managed on lucianomanenti.com.

1. Data Collection

This site is a collection of static tools and games. I do not personally collect or store any personal data of the users.

2. Cookies and Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

3. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to monitor site traffic and improve the user experience. This service may collect anonymous information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited.

Some games also collect anonymous and aggregated statistical information about gameplay (results, duration, game configuration) solely for the purpose of improving the games. This data contains no personal information and cannot be used to identify any user.

4. Managing Privacy Choices

You can manage your consent preferences at any time using the "Manage Cookies" link at the bottom of the page.

5. Contact

For any questions regarding this policy, you can contact me at: postmaster@lucianomanenti.com
