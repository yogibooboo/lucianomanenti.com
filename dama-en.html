How to Play

The game is played on the dark squares with Italian checkers rules. You win by capturing all your opponent's pieces or by blocking every move they have.

Men move diagonally forward, one square at a time. They capture by jumping over an adjacent enemy man, always forward. A man cannot capture a king.

The king (a man that reaches the last row) moves and captures one square in any diagonal direction.

Capturing is mandatory: if you can capture, you must. When several captures are available, the official precedence rules apply: the greatest number of pieces first, then capturing with a king, then the greatest number of kings captured. Multiple captures are played one jump at a time: the game highlights the squares where you can continue.

↶ Undo: takes the game back to before your last move (also Ctrl+Z).
💡 Hint: highlights a good move recommended by the computer.

The game is saved automatically at the start of each of your turns: if you close the page you can resume where you left off.
