About Me

Welcome to lucianomanenti.com. This site was born from my passion for programming as a way to spend my time after retiring.

I am delighted to see that many of you have become passionate about these games, especially Scala 40, and some of you have even sent me touching messages about how these games have helped you during difficult times.

Here you can find several classic card games (Scala 40, Machiavelli, Spider) and a useful tool for musicians: my "quasi-real" metronome.

By popular demand, I am also working on adding Burraco. I have added the relative page marked as "work in progress". I invite you to comment on the layout and the initial game mechanics.
